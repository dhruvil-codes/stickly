The bundled `src/assets/fonts/GiveYouGlory-Regular.ttf` is Give You Glory by Kimberly Geswein.

Copyright (c) 2010, Kimberly Geswein (kimberlygeswein.com).

This font is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The complete license supplied with the font is in [GiveYouGlory-OFL.txt](src/assets/fonts/GiveYouGlory-OFL.txt). A copy of that license and these notices is also included in the desktop bundle's `licenses/` resources directory.

The outlined Stickly wordmark and combined SVG logo are generated from this font. Give You Glory has one supplied weight. The interface uses browser-synthesized bold when the user selects bold text.

## Original Stickly artwork

`public/stickly.svg` is the project's custom sticky note mark. Platform icon files under `src-tauri/icons/` derive from that mark.

## JavaScript and Rust dependencies

Stickly uses Tauri, React, GSAP, Lucide, Tailwind CSS, Vite, TypeScript, and the packages and crates recorded in `package-lock.json` and `src-tauri/Cargo.lock`. Dependencies retain their own licenses. Review the installed package and crate notices when preparing a distribution; this document is not a complete inventory of transitive dependency terms.

## Excluded personal-use assets

The public source and artwork do not include Matcha Salt or its outlined wordmark. Personal-use font files and development captures remain outside version control. They are not a dependency of a fresh checkout.
