# Stickly website

Build a playful, accessible marketing website on GitHub Pages at
https://dhruvil-codes.github.io/stickly/. Its primary action downloads the real
Windows installer. Show the existing product screenshots and films, and let a
visitor try the actual browser renderer. Keep the desktop app unchanged.

The audience wants somewhere visible for quick thoughts and a short checklist,
without opening another planning tool. The site must explain the app in its first
screen, work on a phone, and deliver all important copy as static HTML.

## Argument and proof

- Give an unfinished thought a place: a real editable Stickly sample and download.
- Paper you can move: the existing corner-peel recording, with instructions.
- A note can become a list: real note, checklist, charcoal, and history screenshots.
- It belongs on your desktop: the native Windows recording, pinning and Alt+N.
- Installation is ordinary: the actual setup link and short installation guide.
- The details are clear: visible FAQs, privacy, limitations, and feature reference.

## Art direction

The page is the object, seed 1364177442. On this page, Stickly's seven-color paper
and corner-peel ritual become a desk of oversized notes and filed answer cards.
Keep the brand's Shantell Sans Bold and paper palette, with deep olive ink and a
warm neutral ground. The live thing is the actual React app in a bounded sample
desk. No invented reviews, usage statistics, or unshipped features.

Hero adaptation: the dealt restaurant room and awning stripe have no equivalent
asset in this project. The real note replaces the room, and its compact toolbar
replaces the stripe. The capabilities menu holds real product details; real
screenshots close its other half. The FAQ uses accessible native details cards.
Use only owned product imagery and bundled OFL font assets.

## Copy deck

Hero: Give your brain a little paper.
Stickly is a handwritten sticky note app for Windows. Keep quick thoughts and
little to-dos on your desktop, right where you need them.
Action: Download for Windows. Secondary: Try a note.

Peel: A very satisfying move.
Pull a corner. Lift the whole note. Carry it with your cursor, then click to stick
it somewhere new. A short pull settles back into place.

Features: For your kind of day.
Write freely. Make a list when you need one. Change the paper, keep a note in
front, and bring back something you tucked away.

Desktop: A little space on your desktop.
Keep the next thing in sight while you do the actual work. Pin it above your
windows, or use Alt+N to get it out of the way.

Install: A little setup. Then you're in.
Download the Windows setup, open it, and launch Stickly from Start or your desktop.

FAQ: A few loose ends.
Visible answers cover offline operation, price, Windows support, storage,
plain notes, note pages, and upcoming reminders.

Close: Let it stick.
A good idea. A tiny task. That thing you keep forgetting. Give it a little paper.
