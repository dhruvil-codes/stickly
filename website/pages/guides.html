Which app are you using?

Stickly is separate from Microsoft Sticky Notes and OneNote. Note storage, shortcuts, and recovery steps differ. Stickly does not import or sync Microsoft notes.

For Microsoft notes, start with Microsoft’s getting-started guide or its missing-notes help. For Stickly files and backups, see privacy and storage.
